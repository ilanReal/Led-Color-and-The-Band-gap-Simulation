How it Works

  • Electrons (Cyan): Flowing electrical current.
  • Battery Pump: Lifts electrons up to the top highway to keep the circuit flowing.
  • Conduction Band: The high-energy "highway" where current enters.
  • Valence Band: The lower-energy resting state.
  • Forbidden Zone: Electrons must jump the gap entirely.
  • Photons (Waves): The energy lost in the fall is emitted as light!

Change the Band Gap Slider to alter the drop height. High energy drops create Blue light, low energy creates Red.
