The Apartment Analogy

  • The Ball: An Electron (-).
  • The Ledge: The Conduction Band (The Upper Highway). Electrons here are free to carry current.
  • The Floor: The Valence Band (The Lower Garage). Notice the empty "Holes" (+) waiting for an electron to park.
  • The Drop: The Band Gap. Electrons cannot float in the air; they must jump all the way down.
  • The Sparks: Photons (Light). The energy from the fall is thrown out as a flash of light.

Why the Color Changes

The height of the drop dictates the energy of the photon emitted:

Short Drop: Low Energy = Red Light
Medium Drop: Medium Energy = Green Light
High Drop: High Energy = Blue Light
