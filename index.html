How it Works ▶

  • The Closed Loop: Notice how the electrons travel left along the bottom wire back to the pump. They are endlessly recycled!
  • Battery Pump: Lifts returning electrons up to the top highway, gaining energy.
  • Conduction Band: The high-energy "highway".
  • Valence Band: The lower-energy resting state.
  • Photons (Waves): The energy lost in the single drop is emitted as light!
