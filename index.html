Quantum Dictionary

  • The Ball: An Electron (-).
  • The Ledge: The Conduction Band. You can see electrons flowing freely to carry current.
  • The Floor: The Valence Band. Notice the empty "Holes" (+) waiting for an electron to reach a stable resting state.
  • The Drop: The Band Gap. This is a forbidden zone where electrons cannot exist.
  • The Sparks: Photons (Light). The kinetic energy lost from the fall is released as light waves.

Why the Color Changes

The height of the drop dictates the energy of the photon emitted:

Short Drop: Low Energy = Red Light
Medium Drop: Medium Energy = Green Light
High Drop: High Energy = Blue/Violet Light
