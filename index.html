Quantum Dictionary

  • The Ball: An Electron.
  • The Ledge: The Conduction Band (where electrons are free to move and carry current).
  • The Floor: The Valence Band (a lower, stable energy resting state).
  • The Drop: The Band Gap. Electrons cannot exist in the middle; they must jump all the way down.
  • The Sparks: Photons (Light). The kinetic energy lost from the fall is released as light.

Why the Color Changes

The height of the drop dictates the energy of the photon emitted:

Short Drop: Low Energy = Red Light
Medium Drop: Medium Energy = Green Light
High Drop: High Energy = Blue/Violet Light
